What is Device Owner mode?

Device Owner is Android's official mechanism to make a management app uninstall-proof. When set:

  • Child cannot uninstall GawdZilla Agent
  • Child cannot clear app data
  • Child cannot disable Device Admin
  • Silent app hide/unhide works
  • A permanent notification is always visible — required by Android

How to provision

Pick a fresh enrollment token below and follow one of these methods on the phone.

Method A — ADB on a fresh phone

# no accounts on the phone
adb install GawdZilla-agent.apk
adb shell dpm set-device-owner com.misfit.gawdzilla/.DeviceAdminReceiver

Method B — QR during factory reset setup

  1. Factory reset the phone
  2. At the "Welcome" screen, tap 6 times anywhere
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi
  4. QR scanner appears
  5. Generate the QR on the Build page (next block)

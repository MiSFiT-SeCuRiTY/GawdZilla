Provisioning Steps

  1. Upload the agent APK above (once).
  2. Create a build in the Builder → this generates a fresh token.
  3. Click OPEN QR next to that token.
  4. On the child's phone: factory reset (if it has data/accounts).
  5. During first-boot setup (Wi-Fi screen), tap 6 times on the welcome screen.
  6. QR scanner opens → scan the QR from the page.
  7. Android downloads the APK, sets GawdZilla as Device Owner, completes setup.
  8. The child can no longer uninstall, clear data, or force-stop the agent.
